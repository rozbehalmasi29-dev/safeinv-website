SafeInv Support

Find help with inventory organization, QR codes, and backup and restore.

Getting Started

Open SafeInv and select Personal or Industrial mode. Create storage boxes, add inventory items, and organize them by location.

QR Codes

Use QR codes to identify storage boxes and inventory items. You can display, download, and print supported QR labels.

Backup & Restore

Open Backup & Restore in SafeInv to create a local backup file. Store the backup in a safe location. Restoring an older backup may replace newer inventory changes.

Offline Storage

SafeInv stores inventory locally on your device. If the app is removed or your device is lost, locally stored data may be lost unless you have a separate backup.

Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, or assistance, contact the SafeInv developer.

Report an issue on GitHub

Do not include passwords, personal information, or private inventory data in public issue reports.
