Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SafeInv handles inventory data, device permissions, backups, and user-directed sharing.

1. Local Inventory Storage

SafeInv is designed to store inventory information locally on your device. This may include item names, part numbers, descriptions, quantities, storage boxes, locations, and color preferences. Core inventory management does not require an account.

2. Camera Access

SafeInv requests camera access when needed to scan QR codes. Camera permission is controlled by your device settings. QR scanning is used to identify supported inventory items and storage boxes.

3. Backup and Restore

SafeInv allows you to create local backup files and restore inventory from a selected backup. You are responsible for storing backup files securely. Restoring an older backup may replace inventory changes made after that backup.

4. Sharing and Exporting

When you choose to share or export a file, SafeInv may pass that file to the destination you select through your device's sharing interface. Files shared with other apps or services are subject to those services' privacy practices.

5. Network and Third-Party Services

SafeInv is designed for offline-first inventory management. The current application does not include an account-based inventory synchronization service. Operating system services and third-party components may process technical information according to their own applicable privacy practices.

6. Data Security

SafeInv uses device-local storage for inventory management. You should protect access to your device and keep backup files in a secure location. No storage method can guarantee absolute protection against every form of data loss.

7. Data Deletion

You can manage and delete inventory entries within SafeInv. Removing the application may delete locally stored inventory. Backup files saved outside the app may remain until you delete them from their storage location.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when SafeInv features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact and Support

For questions about SafeInv and this Privacy Policy, visit our support page.
